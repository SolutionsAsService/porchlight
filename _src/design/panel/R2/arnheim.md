# Critic: Formalist (Arnheim), R2

**Anchor used:** Rudolf Arnheim, Art and Visual Perception: light against dark, balance, figure and ground.

**Observed:**
- First screen, light scheme: the R1 defect is resolved. The glow now sits on a near-black night field, so the lit carved features (candle-yellow moon, candy, triangle, BOO) read as the brightest figure against the darkest ground. The pumpkin body is mid-value, which keeps the lit mouth and eyes the focal point.
- Balance: the pumpkin is central and the labels are weighted on both sides. The right column leaves an empty gap between Candy math and Stencil, so the right side is slightly lighter than the left. Minor, not a defect.
- Inner pages (candy, split, dark): each page now holds a dark band with the lantern and the page title, so a figure is present. The band over the cream page is a firm, stable top weight.
- Weakness: the band lantern is only about 90 px wide. In the candy and split shots the single "lit" feature has little value separation from its dark neighbours. On the splitter page I cannot find the lit rib seams at all, and the dark o'clock moon is only faintly brighter than the rest. At this size the "you are here" figure barely separates from its ground.

**Verdict:** PASS. R1 is resolved, and I can point to no concrete defect that blocks.

**Requested change:** Raise the lit feature's value contrast in the band lantern, or scale the lantern up about 1.4x. The one lit feature should be unmistakably the brightest area, and the splitter's rib seams should be visible at that size.

**Statement of meaning:** A small lantern burns in the dark, and each page is the one window of it that is lit.

**One critic question:** If the lit feature cannot be seen at 90 px, does the band still say "you are here", or only "this is a pumpkin"?
