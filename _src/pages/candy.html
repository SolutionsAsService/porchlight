How it's worked out

Buying: kids × pieces each, rounded up to whole bags. Pacing: the kids you expect from now until the light goes off is the rate × the time left; each gets the pieces left divided by that, rounded down, so the bowl can't run out early. It assumes kids arrive at a steady rate, which real nights rarely do (most come early), so treat it as a guide and recheck the bowl. If even one each won't last, it tells you when you'll run out. Both are checked by tests, including one that tries every bowl size from 0 to 120, every 5 minutes up to three hours and rates from 0 to 120 kids an hour, and never promises more than the bowl holds.
