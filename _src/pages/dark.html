What the three times mean

Sunset is when the top of the sun drops below a flat horizon. Dusk (civil dusk) is when the sun is 6° below the horizon: the sky still glows, but cars need headlights and costumes need reflectors. Dark (nautical dusk) is 12° below: the horizon is gone and a flashlight is a must.

How it's worked out

With the sun-position equations NOAA publishes, computed in your browser. Tested against an independent library (astral): within a minute at six test cities on 31 October 2026. The moon is tested against a JPL ephemeris at five dates, within one percentage point, and is shown here for full dark that evening (the front page uses 9 pm). {{tests}} tests run with every build. Hills, buildings and weather can make it darker sooner.

"Use my location" asks your browser for your position, uses it once to do this sum on your device, and does not send it anywhere or keep it.
