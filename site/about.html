About

What Porchlight does, and what it deliberately doesn't.

What it doesn't do

No maps, no lists of houses or streets, no "best spots", no check-ins and no sharing. It never asks where children are or where they're going. Information like that is useful to the wrong people too, so it isn't here at all.

What stays on your device

Everything. The tools are small scripts that run in your browser. If you tap "Use my location" on Dark o'clock, your browser asks you first; the position is used once to compute tonight's dusk and is not sent or stored. Porchlight sets no cookies and loads no analytics, and nothing is fetched from anywhere else: even the fonts are served from this site.

How the numbers are checked

Each tool's arithmetic is in its own file and is tested with every build (8 tests): sunset and dusk against the astral library, the moon against a JPL ephemeris, the candy pacing against every bowl size from 0 to 120 at every 5 minutes and rate, and the haul splitter against 500 random hauls. The code is at https://github.com/SolutionsAsService/porchlight.
