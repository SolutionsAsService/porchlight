Stencil

Turn a name or a word into a printable carving stencil, sized to your pumpkin. The letters are already bridged, so the middles of O and A stay on.

Your word

How to use it

Print at 100% (not "fit to page"), tape it to the pumpkin, prick along the letters with a pin or skewer, peel it off, and cut out what's inside the dots. The dashed oval is the face at the size you chose. Adults do the cutting.

The mouth of the jack-o'-lantern on the front page is the word BOO in this tool's stencil type.
