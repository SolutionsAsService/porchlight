Why no map

Porchlight doesn't list houses, streets or "best spots", and it never asks where children are going. Everything here is about time, quantity and fairness, worked out on your own device for your own eyes. What it does and doesn't do.

Porchlight runs on your device. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking, no maps; nothing you type or share leaves your device, and nothing is loaded from anywhere else. How.
